### Motivation

The statement suggests an association between lower intracellular proline levels and increased resistance to the chemical 2,4-diacetylphloroglucinol (2,4-DAPG) in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Understanding this association could provide insights into the metabolic pathways influencing chemical resistance and the potential role of proline in stress responses.

### Hypothesis

Cells with artificially reduced intracellular proline levels will exhibit increased resistance to 2,4-diacetylphloroglucinol due to altered stress response pathways that are independent of proline's role as an osmoprotectant or antioxidant.

### Experimental Plan

1. **Baseline Conditions:**
   - Use minimal media without amino acids as the base medium for all conditions.

2. **Proline Modulation:**
   - **Control (C1):** No proline supplementation.
   - **Low Proline (C2):** Supplement with 0.5 mM proline to reduce intracellular accumulation.
   - **High Proline (C3):** Supplement with 2.0 mM proline to ensure normal intracellular levels.

3. **2,4-DAPG Treatment:**
   - **Control (T1):** No 2,4-DAPG.
   - **Treatment (T2):** Add 2,4-DAPG at 50 µM, a concentration known to affect yeast viability.

4. **Negative Control:**
   - Supplement with 2.0 mM alanine, as it is not directly involved in the stress response pathways related to proline and does not affect 2,4-DAPG resistance.

5. **Experimental Combinations:**
   - C1T1: No proline, no 2,4-DAPG.
   - C1T2: No proline, with 2,4-DAPG.
   - C2T1: Low proline, no 2,4-DAPG.
   - C2T2: Low proline, with 2,4-DAPG.
   - C3T1: High proline, no 2,4-DAPG.
   - C3T2: High proline, with 2,4-DAPG.
   - Negative Control: Alanine with 2,4-DAPG.

6. **Measurements:**
   - Measure OD600 every 20 minutes to monitor growth.
   - Use mass spectrometry-based metabolomics at 20 hours to confirm intracellular proline levels.
   - Assess viability through growth curves.

### Expected Outcomes

- **C1T2 (No Proline, With 2,4-DAPG):** If the hypothesis holds, these cells should show increased resistance to 2,4-DAPG, reflected in better growth and viability compared to C3T2.
- **C2T2 (Low Proline, With 2,4-DAPG):** These cells may show intermediate resistance, suggesting a dose-response relationship.
- **C3T2 (High Proline, With 2,4-DAPG):** Expect reduced resistance compared to C1T2, indicating that higher proline levels may not confer the same resistance.
- **Negative Control:** Alanine supplementation should not affect resistance, confirming that observed effects are specific to proline modulation.

This experimental design will help determine if lower proline levels correlate with increased resistance to 2,4-DAPG, supporting or refuting the initial statement.
