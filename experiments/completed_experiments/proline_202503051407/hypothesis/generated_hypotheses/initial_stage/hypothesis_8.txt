Understanding this association could provide insights into cellular resistance mechanisms and the role of proline in stress responses.

**Hypothesis:**

Lower intracellular proline levels in Saccharomyces cerevisiae are associated with increased resistance to tirapazamine, potentially due to altered stress response pathways or metabolic changes that enhance cellular survival under chemical stress.
