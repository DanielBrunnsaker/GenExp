   - Control: No acetic acid.
   - Treatment: 50 mM acetic acid.

   - Set up 96-well plates with each combination of glutamine supplementation and acetic acid treatment, including controls.
   - Conditions: 3 glutamine levels x 2 acetic acid levels + 2 negative controls = 8 total conditions.
   - Start all treatments at t=0h and incubate at 30°C with shaking.

6. **Measurements:**
   - Monitor OD600 every 20 minutes to assess growth and viability.
   - Terminate the experiment at 20 hours.
   - Use mass spectrometry-based metabolomics to measure intracellular glutamine levels and other relevant metabolites.

7. **Assumptions Verification:**

   - Ensure acetic acid concentration is physiologically relevant and does not cause complete cell death in all conditions.
