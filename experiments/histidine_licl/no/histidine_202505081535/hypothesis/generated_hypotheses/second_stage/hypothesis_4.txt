### Motivation
The statement suggests an association between lower intracellular histidine levels and decreased resistance to zinc dichloride in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Histidine is an amino acid that can play roles in metal ion binding and homeostasis, potentially influencing cellular resistance to metals like zinc. By modulating histidine levels and observing the response to zinc dichloride, we can explore this association.

### Hypothesis
Cells with reduced intracellular histidine levels will exhibit decreased resistance to zinc dichloride due to impaired metal ion binding or homeostasis mechanisms, potentially leading to increased sensitivity to zinc-induced stress.

### Experimental Plan

#### Media and Conditions
- **Base Media**: Minimal media without amino acids. 
- **Temperature**: 30°C
- **Duration**: 20 hours

#### Supplementations
1. **Histidine Levels**:
   - **Control**: No histidine supplementation.
   - **Low Histidine**: 0.1 mM histidine.
   - **High Histidine**: 1 mM histidine (supraphysiological concentration).

#### Treatment
- **Zinc Dichloride**:
  - **Control**: No zinc dichloride.
  - **Treatment**: 0.5 mM zinc dichloride.

#### Negative Control
- **Valine**: 1 mM valine as a negative control for amino acid supplementation, ensuring it does not affect zinc resistance.

#### Experimental Conditions
1. Control (No histidine, No zinc dichloride)
2. Low Histidine (0.1 mM), No zinc dichloride
3. High Histidine (1 mM), No zinc dichloride
4. Control, Zinc Dichloride (0.5 mM)
5. Low Histidine (0.1 mM), Zinc Dichloride (0.5 mM)
6. High Histidine (1 mM), Zinc Dichloride (0.5 mM)
7. Valine (1 mM), No zinc dichloride
8. Valine (1 mM), Zinc Dichloride (0.5 mM)

#### Measurements
- **OD600**: Every 20 minutes to monitor growth curves.
- **Viability**: Assessed through growth curves.
- **Metabolomics**: Mass spectrometry at experiment termination to measure intracellular histidine levels.

### Expected Outcomes
- **Control Condition**: Normal growth and resistance to zinc dichloride.
- **Low Histidine, No Zinc Dichloride**: Reduced growth due to limited histidine availability, confirming modulation of intracellular histidine levels.
- **High Histidine, No Zinc Dichloride**: Normal or enhanced growth due to excess histidine.
- **Low Histidine, Zinc Dichloride**: Decreased resistance, evidenced by reduced growth compared to control, supporting the hypothesis.
- **High Histidine, Zinc Dichloride**: Potentially normal resistance due to sufficient histidine for metal ion homeostasis, challenging the hypothesis if resistance is not decreased.
- **Valine Controls**: Should show no change in zinc resistance, validating that observed effects are specific to histidine modulation.

This design allows us to test the association between intracellular histidine levels and resistance to zinc dichloride, while controlling for confounders and ensuring the specificity of observed effects.
