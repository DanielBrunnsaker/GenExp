### Motivation

The statement suggests an association between higher intracellular proline levels and decreased resistance to actinomycin D in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Understanding this relationship could provide insights into how proline metabolism affects cellular stress responses, particularly in relation to chemical resistance.

### Hypothesis

Higher intracellular proline levels may lead to decreased resistance to actinomycin D by affecting stress response pathways or cellular homeostasis mechanisms. Proline could potentially influence the expression or activity of genes/proteins involved in detoxification or membrane integrity, thereby altering sensitivity to actinomycin D.

### Experimental Plan

1. **Strain and Media**: Use a reference/wild-type prototrophic strain of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Grow cells in minimal media without amino acids unless specified otherwise.

2. **Supplementation Levels**:
   - **Control**: No proline supplementation.
   - **Low Proline**: Supplement with 1 mM proline.
   - **High Proline**: Supplement with 5 mM proline.

3. **Treatment Levels**:
   - **Control**: No actinomycin D treatment.
   - **Actinomycin D**: Treat with 0.5 µg/mL actinomycin D, a concentration known to stress yeast cells without causing immediate lethality.

4. **Negative Control**: Include a negative control with 5 mM valine, which is not expected to influence actinomycin D resistance, to ensure that observed effects are specific to proline.

5. **Experimental Conditions**:
   - Prepare 96-well plates with the following conditions:
     - 3 proline levels (0, 1, 5 mM) x 2 actinomycin D levels (0, 0.5 µg/mL) = 6 conditions.
     - 2 negative control conditions (5 mM valine with and without actinomycin D).
   - Incubate all wells at 30°C with shaking.
   - Measure OD600 every 20 minutes to monitor growth.
   - Terminate the experiment at 20 hours.

6. **Measurements**:
   - At termination, perform mass spectrometry-based metabolomics to measure intracellular proline concentrations.
   - Evaluate growth curves to assess viability and resistance.

### Expected Outcomes

- **Proline Supplementation**: 
  - Increased intracellular proline levels are expected in the supplemented conditions, confirmed by metabolomics.
  - If proline affects actinomycin D resistance, growth curves should show decreased viability in proline-supplemented conditions compared to controls when treated with actinomycin D.

- **Actinomycin D Treatment**:
  - Cells without proline supplementation should exhibit a baseline resistance pattern to actinomycin D.
  - Any change in resistance in proline-supplemented conditions compared to control will support the hypothesis.

- **Negative Control**:
  - Valine-supplemented conditions should not show significant changes in resistance, validating that proline specifically influences actinomycin D sensitivity.

This experimental setup will allow us to assess whether increased intracellular proline levels correlate with decreased resistance to actinomycin D, potentially supporting the statement's association.
