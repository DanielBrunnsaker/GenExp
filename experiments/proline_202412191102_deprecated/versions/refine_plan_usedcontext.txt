Task:
You are a Saccharomyces cerevisiae systems biology researcher designing batch experiments in a 96-well format. You will be given up to three texts containing variations of a generated hypothesis and experimental plan combination. They are separated with the following string: "####### OPTION X #######", where X is the number of the generated hypothesis. Your goal is to select one of them based on the criteria outlined below.

Selection Criteria:
-Adherence to Constraints: The option should closely follow the initial experimental constraints (see "Experimental Constraints" below).
-Feasibility: The plan should be practical and executable and only use one type of supplementation and/or treatment.
-Logical Implications: The hypothesis and plan should be logically sound.
-Safety: Ensure all treatments and supplementations are safe for laboratory handling.
-Reasonable Supplementations/Treatments: Use appropriate compounds at concentrations likely to effectively test the hypothesis in the specified experimental conditions. 
-Experimental Rigor: The plan should be comprehensive and thorough, and all parts of the plan (such as compound names and concentrations) should be accounted for. All relevant controls should be included in the plan. Ensure the total number of conditions is the product of the number of supplementation levels and treatment levels, plus the treated and untreated negative control.

Experimental Constraints:
-Strain Use: Use only a reference or wild-type strain. Avoid drawing conclusions based on specific genotypes.
-Confounders: Minimize confounders. Verify all assumptions within your experimental plan.
-Experimental Variables: Each variable combination must have its own experiment with appropriate controls.
-Timings: Any supplementation, treatment or condition change must be added at the start of the experiment (t=0h).
-Measurements: Use mass spectrometry-based metabolomics at experiment termination to measure intracellular concentrations/accumulations with standardized protocols. Assume OD600 is measured every 20 minutes. Viability is measured via growth curves.
-Modulating Intracellular Levels: Use physiologically relevant methods based on known S. cerevisiae biology. Justify each method used. Account for its independent effects. Consider compounds or treatments known to specifically affect intracellular metabolite levels in S. cerevisiae.
-Treatment Levels: Specify up to three levels (including control) for each supplementation to evaluate dose-response relationships. Limit yourself to two treatment levels (including the control). Cover a wide range of values to effectively test the statement. Ensure all treatments are at physiologically relevant concentrations. Specify all doses or conditions in detail. Consider the independent effect of each supplementation.
-Baseline Experimental Conditions: Minimal media without amino acids unless specified otherwise. Assume already predefined number of replicates, shaking settings and inoculation concentrations. 
-Experimental Plan Requirements: Provide detailed steps, supplementations, defined concentrations, and temperatures. Denote any added compounds or treatments by their real names. If achieving a target condition, specify the exact treatment to accomplish it. Include experiments to test any assumptions made in your hypothesis or setup. Ensure that the experimental plan uses only one type of supplementation or treatment. Assume termination at 20 hours unless explicitly specified.

Instructions:
-Analyze each option step by step against the selection criteria.
-Score each option, weighing adherence to the experimental constraints highest.
-Select the option with the highest score.
-Explain your choice briefly.
-Output Format: State the selected option as "HYPOTHESIS NUMBER #N", where N is the number of the selected option.
